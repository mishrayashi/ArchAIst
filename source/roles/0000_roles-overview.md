The titles in this field are a mess. Companies use the same word to mean different things. This page gives you the clean mental model so you never feel lost in a job description again.

## The one-line version of each role

| Role | In one line | Core question they answer |
|------|-------------|---------------------------|
| **Data Analyst (DA)** | Explains what *already happened* using charts & SQL. | "What were last month's sales?" |
| **Data Scientist (DS)** | Builds models to *predict* and find deep insights. | "Which customers will churn next month?" |
| **Data Engineer (DE)** | Builds the *pipes* that move & shape data reliably. | "How do we get clean data to everyone, at scale?" |
| **AI / ML Engineer** | Puts ML models into *production* software. | "How do we serve this model to a million users?" |
| **AI Data Engineer** | A DE who also feeds the *AI/ML systems* (features, vectors). | "How do we pipeline data *for AI* reliably?" |
| **GenAI Engineer** | Builds apps on top of generative models (RAG, agents). | "How do we build a chatbot over our company docs?" |
| **LLM Engineer** | Specialises in language models: fine-tuning, serving, evals. | "How do we train/optimise/deploy this LLM?" |

<div class="callout callout-note"><span class="cfor">🧠</span><div><strong>The key split:</strong> Analysts &amp; Scientists mostly <em>use</em> data to produce insight. Engineers <em>build the systems</em> that make data and models usable, fast, and reliable at scale. This academy focuses on the <strong>engineering</strong> side — the roles in highest demand and least crowded.</div></div>

## Which should *you* aim for?

- **Love clean structure, logic, building reliable things?** → **Data Engineer** (best entry point; huge demand; clear path).
- **Already a DE and want to ride the AI wave?** → **AI Data Engineer** then **GenAI/LLM Engineer**.
- **Love maths, statistics, experiments?** → **Data Scientist**.
- **Love software engineering + AI?** → **ML / AI Engineer**.
- **Want to build cool AI apps fast?** → **GenAI Engineer** (most beginner-friendly *AI* role today).

<div class="callout callout-tip"><span class="cfor">💡</span><div><strong>Honest advice for beginners:</strong> Start by becoming a solid <strong>Data Engineer</strong>. It's the most learnable from zero, the most in demand, and it is the foundation for <em>every</em> AI role. You can specialise into GenAI/LLM later — and you'll be far better at it because you'll understand the data underneath.</div></div>

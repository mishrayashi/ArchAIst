The Data Analyst explains what already happened using SQL, dashboards and charts. It's one of the most accessible entry points into the data world — and a great springboard toward Data Engineer later.

## Phase 1 — Data & spreadsheet basics
Understand what data is and get comfortable with Excel/Sheets.
**Study now:** [What is data, really?](#/page/what-is-data) · [Databases & SQL (gentle intro)](#/page/what-is-database)

## Phase 2 — SQL (your #1 skill)
Analysts live in SQL. Get strong here.
**Study now:** [SQL 1 — query like a pro](#/page/sql-basics) · [SQL 2 — windows, CTEs & optimisation](#/page/sql-advanced)

## Phase 3 — Dashboards & visualisation
Power BI / Tableau / Looker. *(Practice with the free desktop tools.)*

## Phase 4 — Business metrics & storytelling
Turn numbers into decisions people act on. *(Learn KPIs, funnels, cohort analysis.)*

## Phase 5 — (Optional) level up to Data Engineer
If you enjoy the technical side, the jump is natural.
**Study now:** [The Data Engineer roadmap](#/page/roadmap-data-engineer)

<div class="callout callout-tip"><span class="cfor"></span><div>Analyst is the fastest role to land from zero, and the SQL you learn here is the same SQL engineers use. Many people start as an analyst and grow into engineering.</div></div>

The AI Data Engineer is a Data Engineer whose pipelines feed AI systems. So you build the full DE foundation first, then add the AI-specific data layer. Each phase links to the exact lessons to study.

## Phase 1 — Become a solid Data Engineer first
This is non-negotiable: AI data work rests on strong data engineering.
**Study now:** [The full Data Engineer roadmap](#/page/roadmap-data-engineer) → work it end to end before continuing here.

## Phase 2 — Understand what AI/ML needs from data
Learn how models consume data so you can shape it correctly.
**Study now:** [What is AI vs ML vs GenAI vs LLM](#/page/ai-ml-dl-genai-llm) · [AI Data Engineer — the role in depth](#/page/role-ai-data-engineer)

## Phase 3 — Embeddings & vector databases
The core new skill: turning text/images into vectors and serving them for search and RAG.
**Study now:** [GenAI 1 — how LLMs work](#/page/genai-llm-basics) · [GenAI 2 — embeddings, RAG, vectors](#/page/genai-rag)

## Phase 4 — Pipelines that feed AI
Reliable ingestion, transformation and orchestration — now aimed at features and training data.
**Study now:** [ETL & ELT](#/page/etl-concepts) · [Spark / PySpark](#/page/spark-basics) · [Orchestration with Airflow](#/page/orchestration-airflow)

## Phase 5 — Quality, drift & governance for AI
Models fail silently on bad/changing data. Own that.
**Study now:** [Data quality & governance](#/page/data-quality-governance) · [Warehouses & lakehouse](#/page/warehouse-basics)

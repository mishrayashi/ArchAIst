The Data Scientist builds models to predict and find deep insight — heavier on statistics and experimentation. This academy is engineering-focused, but several core skills overlap, and we'll be honest about what to learn elsewhere.

## Phase 1 — Maths & statistics foundation
Probability, distributions, hypothesis testing, regression. *(Core to DS; study via a stats course.)*

## Phase 2 — Python + pandas
**Study now:** [Python 1 — the basics](#/page/py-basics) · [Python 2 — pandas & wrangling](#/page/py-data)

## Phase 3 — SQL for analysis
Pulling and shaping data is daily work.
**Study now:** [SQL 1 — querying](#/page/sql-basics) · [SQL 2 — windows & CTEs](#/page/sql-advanced)

## Phase 4 — Machine learning & modelling
**Study now:** [AI vs ML vs DL vs GenAI vs LLM](#/page/ai-ml-dl-genai-llm) — then go deeper with scikit-learn and modelling courses.

## Phase 5 — Communicating insight & trustworthy data
**Study now:** [Data quality & governance](#/page/data-quality-governance) — clean inputs make trustworthy conclusions.

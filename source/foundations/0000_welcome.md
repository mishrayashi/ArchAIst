A few habits that make it stick: **read one lesson then pause** (understanding beats speed), **type every code example yourself** and run it, **mark lessons complete** to watch your progress grow, lean on the **Glossary** for any scary word, and **answer interview questions out loud** before reading the model answer.

## A promise about jargon

This field *loves* jargon. ETL, SCD, HLD, RAG, idempotent, medallion, vector embedding… People use these words to sound smart. We will **always** explain the plain-English meaning first, then the technical term. By the end, *you'll* be the one using them correctly — and you'll know what they actually mean.

## Is this field worth it?

Yes. Data and AI engineering are among the **highest-paid, most in-demand** tech careers in the world, and demand is still growing because every company now collects data and wants to use AI. It's also one of the few high-paying fields you can enter **without a fancy degree** if you can prove your skills.
